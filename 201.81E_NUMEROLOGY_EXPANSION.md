# 201.81E — NUMEROLOGY ENGINE EXPANSION

Status: IMPLEMENTED

This phase upgrades Numerology from a calculator into a structured, versioned evidence producer without adding interpretation.

## Components
- Birth-derived values: Life Path, Birthday, Attitude
- Name-derived values: Expression/Destiny, Soul Urge, Personality, Maturity
- Cycle-derived values: Personal Year/Month/Day
- Pinnacles and Challenges
- Master-number preservation
- Versioned Y-treatment policy
- Repeated-value and master-number pattern clustering
- Structured signal candidates with source scope and calculation lineage

## Separation rule
Number calculation produces structured facts. It does not assign psychological meaning, Theme/Pole direction, relationship state, contradiction, or Fusion Strength.

## Double-counting protection
Repeated numbers are represented as a pattern cluster rather than silently becoming independent evidence.

## Y policy
Y treatment is methodology-configured: CONSONANT, VOWEL, or CONTEXTUAL. CONTEXTUAL currently resolves conservatively to consonant until a versioned phonetic/context rule is introduced.

## Version domains
Methodology and calculation versions remain distinct. Numerology output carries both explicitly.
