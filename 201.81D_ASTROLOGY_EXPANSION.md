# 201.81D — ASTROLOGY ENGINE EXPANSION

Implemented the next Astrology layer without collapsing calculation into interpretation.

## Added
- Explicit structural-signal extraction for planet/sign/house, nakshatra/pada, dignity and retrograde state.
- Time-resolved active Mahadasha lookup.
- Declarative, version-checked YogaDefinition and YogaEvaluation.
- Yoga pipeline: formation → bounded structural strength → context → activation.
- Initial supported formation primitives: SAME_SIGN and MUTUAL_ASPECT.
- Version mismatch, missing-planet and formation-failure reason codes.

## Guardrails
- No planet-keyword shortcut creates a semantic signal.
- Yoga definitions are explicit and methodology-versioned.
- Strength is bounded 0–1 and is not a life-outcome probability.
- Context and activation are separate from formation.
- Interpretation and ontology mapping remain downstream.
- Existing Lahiri/Whole Sign/Parashari calculation layer is preserved.

## Tests
Full suite passes after the expansion.
